# IFRS 18 Transition Brief

Nov 22, 2026 · Nasir Abbas

IFRS 18 replaces IAS 1 for annual periods beginning on or after January 1, 2027, with comparatives restated. Net profit is unchanged; its presentation is not.

**For December year-ends, 2026 is the comparative year, so start account mapping now.** The 2026 annual statements must disclose the expected impact. The Q1 2027 interim is the first IFRS 18 report. In Canada, IFRS 18 applies to Part I (IFRS) reporters.

## What changes

| What changes | What to do now |
| --- | --- |
| **Income statement:** five categories and two new subtotals, operating profit and profit before financing and income taxes. | Map every account; document classification judgments. |
| **MPMs:** adjusted profit measures used publicly move into an audited note, with reconciliations. | List measures in MD&A and press releases; keep only those you can support. |
| **Disaggregation:** fewer "other" lines; function presenters disclose depreciation, amortization, staff costs, impairments and inventory write-downs. | Confirm ledgers and subsidiary packs capture expenses by nature. |
| **Cash flows (IAS 7):** start from operating profit; interest and dividends paid are financing, received are investing. | Restate 2026; warn lenders if operating cash flow moves. |

## Watch-outs

- **Covenants and bonuses** tied to operating profit or operating cash flow may need amending.
- **MPMs become audited;** align them with NI 52-112 non-GAAP disclosures.
- **Subsidiaries,** including ASPE reporters, must supply data by category and by nature.

Source: IFRS 18 *Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements* (IASB, April 2024).
